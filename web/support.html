Install the Windows preview

Download the Windows installer on a Windows 10 22H2 or Windows 11 PC with an Intel or AMD x64 processor. Have your phone nearby for the 20-minute demo.

  1. Run the installer. Setup needs administrator approval. The preview installer is unsigned. The virtual display driver is signed by SignPath Foundation.
  2. Open Phone as a Second Monitor from Start. Connect your PC and phone to the same trusted Wi-Fi network, then scan the QR code on the PC.
  3. Tap Connect device on the phone. On your PC, select Allow touch and keyboard control if you want to interact from the phone, then choose Approve connection.

Drag a window past the edge of your desktop. If the phone stays on the connection screen, open Settings → System → Display on the PC and check whether a second display appears. With just the PC and phone connected, the app can switch Windows to Extend automatically. If Windows still duplicates the screens, press Win + P → Extend and pair again. With several monitors, choose the virtual monitor in Display settings and extend the desktop to it. If the app reports a missing monitor, choose Repair display driver. You can send the output of Copy display diagnostics to support if pairing still fails.

Pair your devices on Mac

On a trusted Wi-Fi network, open the Mac menu bar app and choose Start pairing. Scan the QR code with your phone, choose Connect device and approve the request on your Mac. If local network permission appears, choose Allow. Guest Wi-Fi can prevent devices from reaching each other.

Share your display and enable touch

Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and allow screen recording for the Mac app. Follow the setup guide if macOS asks you to reopen it. For tapping, typing or right-clicking, allow Accessibility and enable touch control in the Mac app. On Mac and Windows, your computer remembers the last touch choice for each browser. You can change it when approving a connection or while connected. A different browser or clearing site data starts you in view-only mode.

Install the Mac download

Open the downloaded ZIP, move Phone as a Second Monitor to Applications and open it. The app runs from the menu bar. The 20-minute demo starts on first launch, so have your phone nearby. You can use the display over the local network during the demo. When it ends, the Mac stops sharing and shows the unlock screen. During the demo, choose Open controls… from the Mac menu bar, then Purchase or restore…. On the purchase screen, choose Unlock for life · €14.99.

How the timer works

The timer counts elapsed time from the first time you open the app. Closing the Mac app, locking your device or disconnecting your phone does not pause the timer. The free demo ends without a charge.

Restore a purchase

For a website purchase, open the app and choose Restore with a recovery code. For a Mac App Store purchase, choose Restore Purchases while signed in with the Apple Account used to buy it.

Where is my invoice?

After a website purchase, MCODE emails your invoice in English as a PDF to the address you enter at checkout. If it is missing, check your spam folder, then contact hello@mcode.hr from that address with your purchase date. For Mac App Store purchases, look for the receipt from Apple.

Lost your website recovery code?

Email hello@mcode.hr with the email used at checkout. If you have it, include your Stripe receipt number or approximate purchase date. We send a verification code to the original checkout address. Reply with that verification code within 30 minutes. Once confirmed, we send a replacement license code to the same address. Save it in your password manager. Keep your card details and license codes private.
