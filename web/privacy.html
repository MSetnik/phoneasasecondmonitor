Privacy

Phone as a Second Monitor is made by MCODE, Matko Setnik. Questions about your data can be sent to hello@mcode.hr.

Display and input

Screen frames, touch gestures and keyboard input travel between your Mac and the device you approve on your local network. Our servers do not receive those frames, gestures or keystrokes. Your computer stores setup and connection preferences locally. The browser stores a random key for this address. Your computer uses its hash to remember whether you allowed touch for that browser. Each connection requires your approval and a fresh session token.

Free demo

Your Mac keeps a local record in Keychain to remember when the 20-minute demo began. The viewer receives the time remaining over your local connection so it can show the countdown.

Mac App Store purchases

Apple handles payment and your Mac checks the signed StoreKit entitlement. The App Store edition does not send license requests to our website. We do not receive your payment card details.

Website purchases

The website edition keeps a random recovery code in your Mac’s Keychain. It sends that code over HTTPS to our Firebase license service when checking or restoring access. The service stores its one-way hash, the associated Stripe payment identifier and entitlement status. Stripe processes checkout information and sends signed payment, refund and dispute notifications to maintain that status. The app periodically verifies access. After a successful check, it can use the cached license for up to seven days offline.

For website purchases, MCODE uses your checkout email, billing details, amount paid and payment method to issue and archive your invoice. MCODE submits the required invoice details to the Croatian Tax Administration for fiscalization and emails the PDF to your checkout address.

If you lose your website license code, support checks the payment in Stripe and uses the original checkout email to verify the request and deliver a replacement. The license service records when the code was replaced for that payment. MCODE uses billing information to provide purchases, handle support and meet applicable accounting obligations. Card details are handled by Stripe. Read Stripe’s privacy policy.

Website and support

Firebase hosts this site and can process technical request information such as IP addresses. This site serves its own fonts and images and uses no advertising trackers or analytics cookies. If you contact us, we use your email and the information you provide to respond. Contact us about access, correction or deletion of your data. Some transaction records may need to be retained for legal obligations.
